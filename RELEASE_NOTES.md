PolyOS 1.2 turns Vara into a full voice and agent assistant, working for you around the clock. It's part of the Developer edition: setup asks "Would you like Vara?", and Settings › Vara adds it any time.

- **Talk to it.** Say "Hey Vera" and ask. Vara answers out loud, takes follow-ups without its name, and "stop" interrupts it. Speech is recognized on your computer; Win+Shift+V is push to talk.
- **A name of your own.** Call it Jarvis, or anything else, in Settings › Vara: it answers to "Hey Jarvis".
- **The HUD.** A full-screen interface with a glowing blue reactor opens when it hears its name (or Win+J), showing what it hears, what it's doing, your system and your schedule.
- **It does things.** Web search and research, driving your browser, music controls, and reminders and routines that run on their own.
- **It knows you.** It remembers your preferences and habits, and searches your own documents, PDFs and notes, all on your computer.
- **It makes its own tools.** Vara writes, tests and keeps new tools for jobs that come up again, and asks an expert model such as Claude when code gets hard.
