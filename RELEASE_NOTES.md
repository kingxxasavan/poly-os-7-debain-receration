PolyOS 1.4.1 fixes installing again from the USB drive, brings the login and lock screens to every screen, lets you pick your web browser, and makes PolyOS next to Windows easier.

- **Installing again works.** On a computer that already had PolyOS, the USB drive's menu started the installed PolyOS after 5 seconds, whatever you picked in the computer's boot menu. The USB drive's own PolyOS is the default now; *Start PolyOS 7 on this computer*, the menu's last line, starts the installed one.
- **Every screen.** A TV or monitor on HDMI shows the login screen too (the same picture as the laptop's screen), and the lock screen covers every screen: the sign-in on your main screen, the blurred lock wallpaper on the others.
- **Pick your web browser.** Setup asks: Google Chrome (Chromium on ARM computers), which comes with PolyOS, or Firefox, which downloads by itself once you're online and takes Chrome's place on the taskbar, in Start, on the desktop and for your links. Settings › Apps › Web browser switches later.
- **PolyOS next to Windows** (the dual-boot download, `polyos-amd64-dualboot.iso`, on this release's page): the PolyOS boot menu now shows at every start with PolyOS, Windows and your computer's firmware settings, and starts PolyOS after 10 seconds. Settings › Power & Performance › *Show the boot menu* hides it again. Start › Power › *Restart to Windows* now asks the computer's firmware to start Windows directly, the way Windows expects, so BitLocker doesn't ask for its recovery key. Before installing next to Windows, turn off Device encryption / BitLocker (Windows 11 turns it on by itself when it's installed) and Fast Startup.
