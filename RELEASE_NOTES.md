PolyOS 1.4.2 fixes what testers found in 1.4.1 and puts the dual-boot download on the website.

- **Setup scrolls properly.** On a small screen, a long step like *Checking your computer* could leave the next step stuck scrolled down. Every step now starts at its top.
- **Usernames.** Names the system uses itself, like *admin*, are caught on the account screen instead of after clicking Install. A name like "Admin" suggests *admin1*.
- **Your own wallpaper.** Settings › Personalization › *Your pictures* shows the pictures in Pictures, Downloads, Desktop and Documents: click one and it's your wallpaper (or sign-in and lock screen picture). Files › right-click › *Set as wallpaper* still works too.
- **PIN.** Settings › Account explains why setting a PIN asks for your password: so only you can set one, not someone who finds your computer unlocked.
- **Dual boot on the website.** The Download page and install guide now offer the dual-boot ISO (`polyos-amd64-dualboot.iso`, advanced) for installing PolyOS next to Windows. Back up your files and turn off BitLocker / Device encryption and Fast Startup in Windows first.
- From 1.4.1: installing again from the USB drive, login and lock screens on every screen, Chrome or Firefox in setup, and the PolyOS boot menu next to Windows.
