PolyOS 1.4 makes setting up a new computer easy: copy one of your others, and install on a drive of its own.

- **Set up like one of your computers.** Connect your Poly Account while installing and pick one of your other computers: the new one gets its settings (taskbar, pinned apps, wallpaper, clock, power), its edition, its theme, and its PolyMarket apps, which install by themselves once it's online. With Poly Sync on, every computer keeps a backup of itself in your account (no files or passwords); Settings › Account shows when it was last backed up.
- **A fresh install.** The download installs PolyOS on a whole drive: pick the drive, and everything on it is replaced. Dual boot next to Windows (and BitLocker and Fast Startup with it) isn't part of the download anymore; the install guide is shorter for it.
- **Fixes.** The screen stays on while PolyOS installs, setup says Google Chrome and Personalization, Start menu search finds Storage and searches like "night light", and taskbar tooltips don't stay behind as empty boxes.
