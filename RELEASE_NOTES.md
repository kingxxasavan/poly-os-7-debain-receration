PolyOS 1.3 is the cleaned-up PolyOS 7: everything tidied up to feel like a finished system. (1.3.1 to 1.3.3: only Google Chrome on the taskbar and desktop, not Chromium too; PolyMarket installs Chrome on computers set up before 1.3; the screen stays on while PolyOS installs from the USB drive; and setup and Start menu search use the new names, like Personalization.)

- **A real Start menu.** Like Windows 11: search apps, settings and your files; pinned apps (right-click to pin to Start or the taskbar); All apps A to Z; your recent files; and power, with *Restart to Windows* on dual-boot computers.
- **A cleaner taskbar.** The launcher button is gone; **^** shows everything running, including apps in the background like Steam or Discord. Quick settings has Airplane mode, Energy saver and Night light next to Wi-Fi, sound and brightness.
- **Only the apps you need.** Screenshot tools, XTerm and other technical extras are gone from your apps. Google Chrome is the browser (Chromium on ARM computers), with Calculator, Photos and Camera built in.
- **Screens made easy.** Plug in a monitor and it just works: Duplicate, Extend or one screen (Settings › Display, or Win+P), remembered and set up again every time, with the wallpaper on every screen.
- **Dual boot that works.** Setup now spots Windows' Fast Startup, which caused the Automatic Repair loop after installing next to Windows (on a D: drive too), and explains how to turn it off. PolyOS starts straight away without a 10-second menu; hold Shift for it, or use Start › Power › Restart to Windows.
- **Sign in your way.** Connect your Poly Account before creating your account, and use it to sign in to the computer with the same name and password. Add a 4 to 6 digit PIN for the sign-in and lock screens; installing and Terminal's sudo still need your real password.
- **Settings, reorganized.** One Account page, Personalization, Apps with Installed, Startup and Usage, a new Storage page, and what closing the lid and the power button do.
- **Drivers from your computer's maker.** Driver Manager shows your model and firmware, installs processor microcode, and gets firmware updates (BIOS/UEFI, SSDs, docks) from Dell, Lenovo, HP and others through the Linux Vendor Firmware Service.
- **More.** Install several PolyMarket apps at once (they queue), drag and drop in Files, *Set as wallpaper* that works for any picture you download, and a tighter security model: other programs can't use your administrator password or reach PolyOS's privileged side.
- **Vara, simplified.** The voice assistant experiment from 1.2 is gone; Vara is back to the chat assistant for apps, settings, code, 3D and robots.
