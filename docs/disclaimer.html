Firmware and drivers

Driver Manager can install drivers and firmware (BIOS/UEFI, SSDs, docks) that come from your computer’s maker through the Linux Vendor Firmware Service, and drivers from Debian and hardware makers. They’re provided by those makers under their own terms. Updating firmware can’t always be undone: keep the computer plugged in, and only update when you need to. Installing another operating system may affect your computer’s warranty or support; check with its maker.
